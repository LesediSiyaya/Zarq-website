How to register your company in 4 steps.

A simple guide to CIPC registration in South Africa.

ZARQ
Swipe →

01

01

Choose a name.

Pick a few name options and reserve one with CIPC. No name yet? You can register with your registration number as the name and add a name later.

ZARQ2 / 6

02

02

Gather your details.

✓ ID numbers of every director

✓ Residential and postal addresses

✓ Email addresses and cellphone numbers

✓ Your company’s address

ZARQ3 / 6

03

03

Register online.

Submit your registration on CIPC’s online platforms, BizPortal or CIPC eServices, and pay the CIPC filing fee.

ZARQ4 / 6

04

04

Get your certificate.

Your registration certificate is sent by email, usually within 1–3 weeks depending on CIPC processing times. Then open a business bank account and you’re ready to trade.

For parents · Save this

5 ways to keep your child safe online.

ZARQ
Swipe →

01

01

Keep devices in shared spaces.

Phones and laptops in the lounge, not the bedroom, especially at night. It keeps conversations open without spying.

ZARQ2 / 7

02

02

Ask what they see, not only how long.

“What made you laugh online today?” opens more doors than “Get off your phone.”

ZARQ3 / 7

03

03

Set privacy together.

Sit with them and make accounts private, turn off location sharing and check who can message them.

ZARQ4 / 7

04

04

Pause before posting.

Teach one simple question: would I be happy if my teacher, my gogo and a stranger saw this?

ZARQ5 / 7

05

05

Make it safe to tell you.

If something goes wrong online, they should know they won’t lose their phone for telling you. Calm first, solutions second.
