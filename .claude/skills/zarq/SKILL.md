# ZARQ

ZARQ is a youth technology and digital opportunity company in Matatiele, Eastern Cape, founded by Lesedi Siyaya.
Two connected sides:
- **ZARQ Digital** (commercial, available now): websites, apps, UI/UX, branding, AI and automation, IT, cybersecurity, digital strategy, CIPC registration, domains. Published prices.
- **ZARQ programmes** (impact, first classes January 2027): free coding, robotics, AI and digital skills classes for ages 5 and up, in Matatiele and online, with Digital Kindness built into every class.

## Ground rules (read first)

1. **Nothing goes live without the owner's "go live".** Plan, build on the working branch, show screenshots, then publish only when she says "go live". Her words: "Do not execute or make any changes without my permission… run the planning past me and then we'll execute together."
2. **Brand name is always ZARQ in capitals.** Never "Zarq" or "zarq" in text. Legal name exactly: **ZARQ (PTY) LTD**. Addresses stay lowercase: zarq.co.za, admin@zarq.co.za, @zarq_sa.
3. **Be honest about what exists.** Classes start January 2027; never say classes have run, never invent learner numbers, outcomes, partners, testimonials or quotes. Year-one figures are goals, not results. B-BBEE is from an affidavit: say "Level 1 B-BBEE contributor", never "certified".
4. **No visible disclaimers on stock imagery** (she dislikes them: "we don't want to give an impression of a demo website"). Keep captions truthful instead. The AI-generated class video on the Partners page keeps its small label because sponsors read that page.
5. **Children on camera:** stock photos only until classes start; after that, written parental consent first.
6. Write plain, warm, professional South African English. No "not X, it's Y" drama, no startup-apologising tone: present ZARQ as a company with a clear goal.

## The owner's working style

- Short replies, numbered choices. She answers with "1", "2", "yes", "go live".
- Shows screenshots of problems; fix what she points at, nothing more.
- Wants things that look premium and captivating but stay on-brand.
- Keep files she will need (videos, images) in a download she can save; scratch folders get wiped when sessions restart.
